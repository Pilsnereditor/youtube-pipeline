YouTube Login Setup

Follow the 3 steps to authorize all your channels for the pipeline

1
2
3

Step 1 — Select Profile

A Chrome profile stores your YouTube login cookies so the pipeline can upload automatically. Create one per Google account.

Loading profiles...

Step 2 — Log Into YouTube

A live Chrome browser will open below. Follow the steps to authorize your channels.

Launching Chrome...
①

If Chrome asks to use your existing profile

Click "Don't sign in" or "Cancel" — do NOT open a separate Chrome profile. This keeps the session inside the app's own profile folder.

②

Sign in to your Google / Gmail account

Use the account that owns all your YouTube channels. Navigate to YouTube and make sure you're logged in.

③

Make sure YouTube is in English

The pipeline needs English UI. If it's not in English, click your profile icon → Language → English. Change it before clicking Verify.

④

Click "Verify Channels" below and wait

This checks your login, detects all channels, and verifies the language. It may take 30–60 seconds — don't close the browser.

Step 3 — Verification Results

Review the check results below. Fix any issues, then save to finalize.
